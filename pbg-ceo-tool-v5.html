Section 02

Scoring Logic

D1 — Vivo Brand Strength · 60 Points Total

D1 measures what Vivo has built inside the store — independently of any competitor. 60 points because physical brand presence is the foundation. Without it, no competitor battle matters. Asset Tier carries 30 because the type and scale of investment signals commitment. Position carries 10 because first-contact with the customer is disproportionately valuable. Visibility Branding carries 20 because it drives awareness before the customer even reaches the counter.

Asset Tier · 30 Pts

TierConditionPoints
Tier 1Shop-in-Shop / X-Zone / Shop Upgrade / Customized Zone present30
Tier 2No project · 2+ Experience Cabinets20
Tier 3No project · 1 Experience Cabinet12
Tier 0No qualifying assets0

Tier 1 — Vivo has built its own dedicated zone inside the store. This is financial commitment, not just presence. A competitor cannot simply place a standee next to it and compete.

Tier 2 — Vivo is present with multiple premium cabinets. Credible, but displaceable.

Tier 3 — Vivo has a foothold. One cabinet means one competitor push can remove us.

Tier 0 — Vivo does not exist in this store's premium asset landscape.

Asset Position · 10 Pts

PositionScoreMeaning
Position 110 ptsFirst asset customer sees on entry
Position 27 ptsSecond contact point
Position 34 ptsThird contact point
Position 4+1 ptBeyond primary attention zone
Hidden / Absent0 ptsNo customer reach

Customer attention follows a decay curve from store entry. Position 1 captures the highest purchase-influence moment. Each step back is not linear — it is compounding loss. A Tier 1 asset at Position 4 loses most of its investment value. Position is scored steeply to reflect this reality.

Visibility Branding · 20 Pts

  • In-shop presence5 pts
  • Out-shop presence5 pts
  • In-shop sq.ft range5 pts
  • Out-shop sq.ft range5 pts

Visibility branding drives awareness before the customer reaches any asset. In-shop branding reinforces the decision at point of purchase. Out-shop branding intercepts the customer before they enter. Space matters because a 10 sq.ft sticker and a 150 sq.ft backwall are not the same signal — to the customer or to the retailer.

D2 — Vivo vs Competitor Fighting Score · 40 Points Total

D2 measures Vivo's position relative to Samsung and Apple — head-to-head, parameter by parameter. 40 points because relative dominance determines which brand the store staff instinctively recommend. D1 tells us what Vivo has. D2 tells us whether it is enough to win.

Battle Structure

Two battles: Vivo vs Samsung · Vivo vs Apple

D2 final = average of both battle scores.

  • Competitor absent from store → Vivo wins that battle = 40 / 40
  • Both brands absent on a parameter → score = 0 for that battle
  • Final = (Samsung battle + Apple battle) ÷ 2

Each battle is fought across 3 parameters. On each parameter: Vivo higher = Win. Equal = Draw (0.5). Competitor higher = Loss (0). Final battle score = (Wins + 0.5 × Draws) ÷ 3 × 40. If a competitor is not present in the store at all — Vivo wins that battle by default. You cannot lose a fight your opponent didn't show up to.

3 Parameters

#ParameterWhat is compared
1Asset PresenceProjects + Experience Cabinets — Vivo weight vs competitor weight
2Asset PositionCustomer reach sequence — Vivo position vs competitor position
3Visibility BrandingIn-shop + Out-shop sq.ft combined — Vivo vs competitor

These 3 parameters cover the complete customer influence chain: what is physically present (Asset Presence), where it sits in the customer journey (Asset Position), and how much visual territory Vivo owns (Visibility Branding). Every other variable is either a subset of these or too subjective to score consistently across 30 stores.

Tiers are fixed score bands — not relative rankings. A store's tier reflects its actual performance, not how it compares to other stores this month.

Tier A · Dominant (75–100): Vivo leads on assets and visibility against both Samsung and Apple. Protect and showcase these stores.

Tier B · Competitive (50–74): Vivo is present and holding ground but not leading. Maintain investment and push for upgrade.

Tier C · Low Visibility (25–49): Vivo is physically present but outgunned. Competitor controls the store narrative. Urgent upgrade needed.

Tier D · Poor Visibility (0–24): Minimal Vivo presence. This store is effectively competitor territory. Critical intervention required.
