Section 02

The Framework

Why This Assessment Exists

01

Past reports showed Vivo present in store. That was not enough information.

02

This framework measures dominance — not just presence. These are not the same thing.

03

The result: a score every upgrade decision can be traced back to. No opinion. No assumption.

Benchmark: 60/100. Today’s average: 43/100.

Presence ≠ Dominance

D1

Vivo Brand Strength

Max 40

Measures every Vivo branding asset present in the store.

D2

Competitive Pressure Index

Max 30

Measures competitor branding pressure in the same store.

D3

Store Premium Potential

Max 30

Measures whether the store can drive premium Vivo sellout.

3-question test — remove any one dimension and the decision breaks

Dimension Question It Answers Why It Cannot Be Removed
D1 — Vivo Brand Strength Where are we today? Without this, we don't know our current state
D2 — Competitive Pressure Index Who are we fighting and how hard? Without this, our score has no competitive context
D3 — Store Premium Potential Is this store worth fighting for? Without this, upgrade decisions have no ROI logic
